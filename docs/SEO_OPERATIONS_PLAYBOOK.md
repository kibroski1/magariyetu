# Magari Yetu SEO Operations Playbook

This playbook covers the remaining non-code parts of Steps 6–13. Complete every required item before treating the marketplace as launch-ready.

## 1. Structured-data review — required

1. Test an active listing, a sold listing, a make/model page, a dealer page, one article, and one guide with Google’s Rich Results Test.
2. Confirm that vehicle price, availability, mileage, images, and breadcrumbs match what the visitor sees.
3. Never mark invented FAQs or fabricated reviews as structured data.
4. Re-test after changing listing, pricing, or editorial templates.

## 2. Editorial publishing — required

1. In Payload Admin, create articles and guides only with a real author name, update date, original body copy, and cited sources for factual claims.
2. Start with these priority intents: Toyota Harrier price in Kenya; Toyota Harrier 2020 price Kenya; Toyota Axio price Kenya; best SUVs in Kenya; cars under KSh 1 million; best family cars; importing from Japan; KRA import duty; car registration costs; Toyota vs Subaru; and used-car buying.
3. Link each guide to relevant current listings and model pages. Do not publish thin or near-duplicate AI pages.
4. Refresh market, tax, registration, and import guidance whenever the source information changes.

## 3. Local SEO — required where Magari Yetu has a real presence

1. Claim and verify a Google Business Profile only for a genuine, eligible Magari Yetu location. Use the same name, address, phone number, hours, and website everywhere.
2. Publish a county page only after it has at least three active local listings and useful local context. The application enforces the three-listing indexing threshold.
3. Do not clone city pages by changing only a place name.

## 4. Trust and moderation — required

1. Keep the About page, contact route, Terms, Privacy Policy, and Marketplace Rules accurate and visible.
2. Moderate every reported listing and keep dealer/private-seller identity signals accurate.
3. Do not publish fabricated reviews or misleading verification claims.
4. Have Kenyan-qualified legal counsel review the legal pages and replace every bracketed business placeholder before launch.

## 5. Authority — required, but manual

1. Build legitimate relationships with Kenyan automotive blogs, newsrooms, reviewers, creators, dealers, mechanics, insurers, lenders, importers, directories, and automotive communities.
2. Earn mentions by publishing data and tools worth referencing—not by buying links or using link schemes.
3. A strong first asset is a Kenya Used Car Price Index based on responsibly aggregated Magari Yetu listing data. Explain the sample, method, date, and limitations.

## 6. Automation and AI — required approach

1. Keep routine operations deterministic: slugs, taxonomy keys, metadata defaults, canonical URLs, breadcrumbs, schema, sitemaps, internal links, and index eligibility.
2. Treat AI as optional assistance only for messy seller descriptions, possible feature extraction, draft improvement, FAQ ideas, and uncertain categorisation.
3. Review AI-derived details before publishing. Do not let AI invent specifications, price history, legal facts, or seller claims.

## 7. Google Search Console and continuous improvement — required

1. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` to the Google Search Console verification token and deploy.
2. Verify the property, submit `/sitemap.xml`, and review it after every release.
3. Weekly: inspect indexing exclusions, crawl errors, Core Web Vitals, structured-data issues, and mobile usability.
4. Monthly: review queries, impressions, clicks, click-through rates, organic listing views, enquiries, dealer leads, and seller leads.
5. Improve pages that have impressions but poor click-through rate with accurate titles/descriptions. Improve pages that receive clicks but do not convert with better inventory, trust information, and enquiry flow.
6. Quarterly: remove or noindex thin/duplicated pages, refresh high-value content, and set the next content and authority priorities from Search Console and conversion data.
