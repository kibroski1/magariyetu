# Magariyetu marketing kit

Use one primary message per asset. Do not describe paid placement as verification, and do not promise inspection, finance, or buyer protection until the service is live.

## Brand line

**Find it. Check it. Drive it.**

Supporting line: “Kenya’s smarter vehicle marketplace for cars, work vehicles, machinery and parts.”

## Brand mark

Use the car-and-rising-arrow mark in `public/brand/magariyetu-mark.png` whenever the logo is shown. Its colours are fixed to Magariyetu's customs-ledger navy (`#10233F`), clearance-stamp amber (`#C98A2B`), and logbook paper (`#F6F4EE`). Keep the mark intact: do not redraw, crop into the car, recolour individual parts, or add effects. When sharing either business-card SVG outside this project, keep the adjacent `brand/` folder with it so its linked mark remains available.

## Ready-to-use social copy

### Buyer post

Looking for your next car, truck, machine or spare part? Compare real listings, estimate import duty and contact sellers directly on WhatsApp. Start at **magariyetu.co.ke**.

### Dealer recruitment post

Put your stock where serious buyers can find it. Build a dealer storefront, manage inventory and see the leads each listing creates. Join Magariyetu today.

### Trust post

Before you send money: inspect the vehicle, confirm the seller, check the logbook and ask questions. Magariyetu is built to help you make a better decision before the first call.

## Business cards

The files in `public/marketing/` are print-ready SVG layouts at a 1050 × 600 ratio. Give a printer the final PDF after replacing **YOUR NAME**, the contact details and the QR-code block. Print at the standard 85 × 55 mm finished size with 3 mm bleed added by the printer.

- `business-card-front.svg`: logo/brand side.
- `business-card-back.svg`: staff contact side.

## First launch campaign

1. Recruit 20 legitimate dealers in one launch area, offering hands-on free onboarding.
2. Publish three buyer-help videos weekly: viewing checklist, import-duty walkthrough, and scam warning.
3. Give each dealer a QR card linking to their storefront or the dealer onboarding page.
4. Retarget only consented visitors; do not upload phone lists or WhatsApp contacts into ad audiences without valid consent.
5. Review weekly: new active listings, lead rate, dealer activation, repeat visitors and cost per approved dealer.
